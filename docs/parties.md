# Parties (server design)

Server-side rules for parties. The client contract is [Party protocol](party-protocol.md).

## Parties

Party play: players form a **party** (the one term in code, packets, commands and messages) and share a procedural instance, its kills, its loot and its experience. `docs/party-protocol.md` is the client contract.

- **`PartyService`** (`Avalon.World/Parties`, a DI singleton, World-side; nothing about parties is on `Avalon.World.Public`) holds every party **in memory only**: a restart disbands them all, and a party lives on one world server (no cross-world party). **Tick thread only, every method**: the six handlers, the party commands, `World.SpawnInInstance`/`TransferPlayer`/`LeaveWorldAsync`, `World.Update` (`Tick`) and `WorldServer.Update` (`FlushMemberStatus`) all call it on the tick. No timers: every deadline is read from the container's `TimeProvider` inside those calls. A `Party` (`PartyId`, a `ValueObject<uint>` numbered from 1 per run; `Leader`; `Members` in join order, so the first is the longest-standing; `ExperienceMode`; `ExperienceModeLockedUntil`) is changed only by the service. A character is in at most one party, and `CharacterEntity.PartyId` (World-side, `internal set`) mirrors it for `Hostility`. A member who logs out stays a member, shown offline with its last known level; `CharacterOnline` (from `SpawnInInstance`) sets or clears its `PartyId` and sends its party the roster, and `CharacterOffline` (from `LeaveWorldAsync`, contained, before the logout save) sends the others the roster.
- **Requests and their answers** (a `PartyResult` each; refusals listed in the order they are checked):
  - `Invite(inviter, name)`: the name is looked up among online characters, ignoring case and surrounding spaces. `NotFound` (the inviter is not online), `NotLeader` (the inviter is in a party it does not lead), `NotFound` (the target is not online), `Self`, `AlreadyInParty`, `InvitePending` (the target already holds one), `PartyFull` (at `MaxPartySize`). An invite that passes sends the target `SMSG_PARTY_INVITE` and expires after `PartyInviteTimeoutSeconds`; one to a target that ignores the inviter is answered the same and never sent (see the ignore list under Chat Commands, #723). No party exists until an invite is accepted: an inviter with none leads the one formed then.
  - `Respond(target, accept)`: `NoInvite` without one; the invite is spent either way. A decline tells the inviter `InviteDeclined`. An accept is refused `AlreadyInParty`, `NotFound` (the invite's party is gone, the inviter now sits in a party it does not lead, or it went offline before any party existed) or `PartyFull`; otherwise the target joins: roster to every online member and "X joined the party.". An expiry tells both sides `InviteExpired`, naming the other; a character going offline ends every invite it sent or holds, the other side told `InviteExpired`.
  - `Leave(member)`: `NotInParty` or Ok. `Kick(leader, member)` and `Promote(leader, member)`: `NotInParty`, `NotLeader`, `Self`, `NotFound` (not a member, or, for a promote, offline: `Tick` would hand leadership straight back). A leave or a kick tells the others "X left the party." / "X was removed from the party.", sends the removed character an empty roster and clears its `PartyId`, and gives it the leave countdown when it stands in that party's instance.
  - `SetExperienceMode(leader, mode)`: `NotInParty`, `NotLeader`, `Invalid` (mode `Unknown`), `OnCooldown` (within `PartyExperienceModeCooldownSeconds` of the last switch), `InCombat` (any online member `IsInCombat`). The mode already set is Ok and changes nothing (the cooldown and combat are checked first); a switch applies at once, restarts the cooldown, and sends the roster and "Experience is now shared evenly." / "Experience is now shared by level.". `Even` is the default.
- **Leadership and disband.** A promote, and every handover, sends the roster and "X is now the party leader.". A leader that leaves, goes offline, or is found offline by `Tick` hands leadership to the longest-standing **online** member; with nobody else online a leader that went offline keeps it, and one that left hands it to the longest-standing member, offline as it is (`Tick` then passes it on once a member is online). A character deleted while in a party stays on the roster (offline) until the party disbands or the server restarts; the `CMSG_CHARACTER_DELETE` handler does not touch parties. Fewer than 2 members disbands the party: `IPartyInstanceRegistry.ForgetParty`, every member detached (empty roster, `PartyId` null) and told "The party was disbanded." (one inside the party's instance gets the countdown line as well).
- **Hostility.** Two members of one party are never hostile, whatever their PvP flags (`Hostility.IsHostile`, `SameParty` on `CharacterEntity.PartyId`), so an `Affects = Ally` heal reaches them unchanged.
- **Party instances.** `IPartyInstanceRegistry` (`Avalon.World/Instances`, World-side, `World.PartyInstances`; `InstanceRegistry` implements it, and the modding API's `IInstanceRegistry` does not have it). In `EnterMapHandler` (which first refuses any portal entry while a move is under way, `MoveInProgress`, see Item use) step 8 a character in a party whose portal leads to a Normal map goes to `GetOrCreatePartyInstanceAsync(party, map)`: an index party → map → instance, with the build de-duplicated per (party, map) as for #442 and published on the tick like every build (see InstanceRegistry), so members entering at once share one instance. `MapInstance.OwnerPartyId` is set at construction (init-only) and a party instance has no `OwnerCharacterId`. Once the build returns the handler re-checks that the character is still in that party (otherwise `MapTransitionResult.MapNotFound`) and that `PlayerCount` is below `min(MaxPartySize, map.MaxPlayers ?? MaxPartySize)` (otherwise `InstanceFull`, 6; so a procedural map must seat at least `MaxPartySize`: the seeded forest, map 2, holds 6, migration `RaiseForestMaxPlayers`, #707, after a seed of 1 let only the first member in, and `SeedIntegrityShould` pins every seeded Normal map); continuations run one after another on the tick and `TransferPlayer` adds at once, so two members arriving in one tick are counted right. Outside a party nothing changed (the character's own instance and its re-entry window); joining a party leaves a character's solo instances alone, but its portals no longer lead to them. Towns are unchanged. **Login always lands in a town** (owner decision; `CharacterSelectHandler`), never back in a party instance: a member re-enters through a portal. A disband forgets the party's index at once (a build published after the forget, wherever it finished, stays unindexed); its instances empty and expire on the usual rule (`Game:AbandonedInstanceLifetimeMinutes` once entered and emptied, see [instanced maps](instanced-maps.md#expiry-cleanup)), and since `MapInstance` stamps `LastEmptyAt` at construction, an instance nobody ever entered expires too, after a fixed 15 minutes. A new leader changes nothing.
- **Leave countdown.** A character removed from a party (leave, kick, disband) while online in an instance that party owns (`IsPartyInstance`, which reads `OwnerPartyId` and so still holds after `ForgetParty`) gets a countdown of `PartyLeaveGraceSeconds`: at once "You left the party." / "You were removed from the party." / "The party was disbanded." followed by " Returning to town in 60 seconds.", then "Returning to town in N seconds." at 30, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 (marks at or above the grace are skipped; a late tick announces only the lowest mark it passed). `PartyService.Tick`, run by `World.Update` before the instances tick, returns the connections whose deadline passed while still in that instance; `World` hands them to `PartyService.StartReturns`, which starts `TownReturn` (`Avalon.World/Respawn`, the move `RespawnAtTownHandler` uses) with `dropEncounter: true` and `revive: IsDead`, setting `RespawnInFlight` (a connection already returning is not started twice, see below), and the move goes through `World.TransferPlayer`; a character that died on the way arrives revived too. `TownReturn` settles both of its tasks (the town lookup and the town instance) before its continuations, as `CommandContext.Then` does, so a lookup or build that faults, or a step that throws, clears `RespawnInFlight` (only while the connection still holds that character) rather than leaving it set for good, and is logged at Error, or handed to the caller's `failed` callback when it passes one (`RespawnAtTownHandler` passes none). **A failed countdown return is retried (#700):** `StartReturns` passes `failed` as `PartyService.ReturnFailed`; the service remembers each return under way (forgotten by `InstanceChanged`, so a transfer that happened settles it, and on going offline) and, while the character is still online, still in the instance the countdown was for and not back in that party, re-arms a silent countdown of `Game:PartyReturnRetrySeconds` (default 5, 1-3600) with no marks announced, logging the failure at Warning; after `PartyService.MaxReturnRetries` (5) retries, or when those conditions no longer hold, the failure is logged at Error and nothing more is tried. A countdown that runs out while `RespawnInFlight` is already set (a respawn under way) is re-armed the same way rather than started twice, and a `TownReturn` that cannot be resolved counts as a failure for each. The player is told nothing more after the countdown reached zero. The party tick and each return are contained in `World.Update` (the tick's throws logged at most once per 10 s, `ThrottledErrorLog`), and so are the party calls in `SpawnInInstance`, `TransferPlayer`, `WorldServer.Update` (`FlushMemberStatus`, throttled the same way) and the kill's eligibility (`CreatureKilled` falls back to nobody eligible), so a party throw never costs the instances their tick, the online row, a transition or a corpse its teardown. It is cancelled by joining the same party again, by being in any other instance when checked (a portal, a respawn in town, any transfer) and by going offline. During it the character shares no kill (below) but still counts for health scaling.
- **Health scaling.** Party instances only. `MapInstance.HealthFactorFor(players, PartyHealthPerExtraPlayer)` = `1 + perExtra × (players − 1)`, 1 for one player or none; players are every character in the instance, the dead and those in a countdown included. `AddCharacter`/`RemoveCharacter` only mark it owed; the start of the instance's next `Update`, before the creature scripts, recomputes once, so several changes in one tick make one rescale and one message. When the factor changed, each creature gets `Creature.Rescale(factor)` (World-side, not on `ICreature`): maximum `round(BaseMaxHealth × factor)`, at least 1, `BaseMaxHealth` fixed by `CreatureSpawner` at spawn; current health keeps its share (`CharacterStatsCalculator.KeepShare`); a creature at 0 health is never rescaled (#672). Every connection in the instance gets one system line, "Creatures now have 220% health (3 players).", prefixed with "X has entered." or "X has left." when exactly one arrival or departure caused it. A creature spawned later starts at the current factor. The `Health` mark rides the creature's next update broadcast (`DescribeUpdatedObject` adds `Health` and `IsDead` to `CreatureUpdate` when marked), so clients already watching see the new maximum. Damage, armour, experience and loot are not scaled.
- **Kills.** `MapInstance.CreatureKilled` takes `PartyEligibility.For` once, first, on the tick, while the creature is still in its encounter (pure): a killer that is not a character or is in a countdown makes nobody eligible; a killer in no party is eligible alone; in a party, every member present in this instance, alive or dead, not in a countdown, and in the creature's encounter or within `PartyEligibilityRange` of the corpse on X/Z, and the killer always. It applies wherever the kill happens. **Loot**: `PartyLootAllocator` (see the ground-loot bullet) draws one eligible member per drop, uniformly through `ICombatRandom.NextInt64(0, n − 1)` (no draw for one), reserved for `LootGracePeriod`; with nobody eligible a party instance's drop is free for all at once. **Experience**: `PartyExperience.Split(creature.Experience, creature.Level, eligible, mode, PartyExperienceBonusPerExtra, PartyExperienceLevelGap, member => ExperienceAward.CanGainExperience(member, data))`, pure: a member at `creature level + gap` or more, or one that can gain no experience (at the maximum level or before a gap in the levels, #735, owner decision), gets nothing and is not counted, **solo too** (a character 5 levels above a creature earns nothing from it, and the others share the whole pool); n = 1 takes everything with no bonus; otherwise the pool is `xp × (1 + bonus × (n − 1))`, `Even` gives `floor(pool / n)` each and `LevelWeighted` `floor(pool × level / Σ levels)`. The mode is the first eligible member's party's. Each share then goes through the map's level band for that member (`ScaledExperience`) and the usual level-up (`Refill`, or `KeepShare` for a dead member); a level-up resends the roster (`LevelChanged`).
- **Protocol.** Six requests, each with a `MapSessionFilter` entry and a handler in `Avalon.World/Handlers`: `CMSG_PARTY_INVITE` (0x20B0, `TargetName`), `_INVITE_RESPONSE` (0x20B1, `Accept`), `_LEAVE` (0x20B2), `_KICK` (0x20B3, `CharacterId`), `_PROMOTE` (0x20B4, `CharacterId`), `_EXPERIENCE_MODE` (0x20B5, `Mode`). Each runs through `PartyReplies.Handle` and gets **exactly one** `SMSG_PARTY_RESULT` (0x30B1: `PartyResult`, append-only, and the name involved where known), sent after any roster or line the request caused; a request that throws is logged at Error and answered `Error` (15); a connection with no character gets nothing. `SMSG_PARTY_INVITE` (0x30B0) carries the inviter's name, class, level and `ExpiresInMs`. `SMSG_PARTY_ROSTER` (0x30B2: `PartyId`, `ExperienceMode`, `ModeLockedForMs`, members with `CharacterId`, `Name`, `Class`, `Level`, `IsLeader`, `Online`, `SameInstance` relative to the recipient) goes to every online member on a join, a leave, a kick, a leader change, a mode switch, a member coming online or going offline, any member's instance change (`World.TransferPlayer` calls `InstanceChanged`, so every map entry and respawn) and a level-up; an empty one (`PartyId` 0, no members) tells a character it is in no party. `SMSG_PARTY_MEMBER_STATUS` (0x30B3: health, maximum, power, maximum, power type, dead) is `FlushMemberStatus`, run by `WorldServer.Update` after `AbilityAmountsFlusher`: each online member whose values differ from what was last sent for it, and last sent at least `PartyService.StatusInterval` (250 ms) ago, goes to the other online members in its own instance, never to itself; the memory is dropped on a join, an instance change and a login, so they are sent afresh. The retired group opcodes (`CMSG_GROUP_INVITE_RESULT` 0x200A, `SMSG_GROUP_INVITE` 0x300D, `SMSG_GROUP_INVITE_RESULT` 0x300E) were deleted from `NetworkPacketType` (#697), and their numbers are free.
- **Commands** (`Avalon.World/Parties/PartyCommands.cs`, Player access): `/invite <name>` (`/inv`), `/leave`, `/kick <name>`, `/promote <name>` and `/partyxp even|level` make the same `PartyService` calls and are answered with the same `SMSG_PARTY_RESULT`, plus a system line on a refusal (`PartyReplies.Describe`) and "You invited X to the party." on an invite; kick and promote take a name among the caller's party members. `/p <message>` (`/party`) sends the text as typed (after the command word, trimmed; leading spaces and slashes skipped as the dispatcher skips them; none is answered "Usage: /p <message>") to every online member of the sender's party, the sender included, on `ChatChannel.Party`, wherever they are.
- **Settings** (`GameConfiguration`, range-validated at startup): `MaxPartySize` 6 (2-40), `PartyInviteTimeoutSeconds` 60 (1-3600), `PartyLeaveGraceSeconds` 60 (1-3600), `PartyReturnRetrySeconds` 5 (1-3600), `PartyExperienceModeCooldownSeconds` 60 (0-86400, 0 turns it off), `PartyHealthPerExtraPlayer` 0.6 (0 or more), `PartyEligibilityRange` 60 m (1 or more; its own setting, with `InterestRadius`'s default), `PartyExperienceBonusPerExtra` 0.10 (0-10), `PartyExperienceLevelGap` 5 (1-1000).
