# Auras (server design)

Server-side rules for auras. The client contract is [Aura protocol](aura-protocol.md).

## Auras

Auras: timed effects on a character or a creature, periodic damage or healing, stat modifiers (buffs, debuffs, slows), or both, put on by abilities, item scripts and aura scripts. A harmful aura can also put its holder under crowd control: a stun, a root, a silence or a slow ([Crowd control](#crowd-control-770)). No dispel yet. `docs/aura-protocol.md` is the client contract.

### Arrival protection (#1088)

Every living character receives **Arrival Protection** (reserved aura 11) on world entry and map arrival,
a helpful buff lasting **60 seconds**. Login and reconnect keep the existing readiness barrier; the protection starts
when the character actually enters the instance. Portals and respawns grant a fresh copy. The usual aura list/update
packets display its countdown, and the player can cancel it through `CMSG_AURA_CANCEL`.

The protection prevents enemy targeting, creature aggro, direct and periodic damage, and new harmful auras. Helpful
area effects can still affect the character. It protects the holder only as a target: a protected character cannot
act without ending it, and an aura it put on others before it arrived still ticks, threatens and tags combat as usual.
The buff ends when its character changes horizontal position, when the cast system takes a cast of its (cast bar or
instant, item casts included: `InstanceAbilityCastSystem.QueueAbility`/`RunInstant`, before the start is sent or the
cast fires), when an item use of its passes its checks (a script use, a cast bar, an equip or unequip: `ItemUseService`,
with the item and doodad cast interrupts), when a drag of its equips or unequips gear (`ItemMoveHandler`, an accepted
`CMSG_ITEM_MOVE` touching Equipment), when a doodad use of its passes its checks (instant or cast bar:
`DoodadUseService.Interact`, with the same interrupts), when its 60 seconds expire, or when its owner cancels it. A
refused cast, use, drag or interaction keeps it, and so does a drag that touches no gear. Turning, idle input and pressing into a wall without moving do not end it. A portal or teleport removes
the old protection when leaving or repositioning and grants fresh protection on arrival.

The buff is seeded with the other auras and loaded through `StaticData.Auras`. Arrival applies its ID through the
destination instance's `AuraSystem`, using the loaded duration and metadata; a missing or refused row grants no
protection and uses the ordinary throttled refusal log. The seeded row has no script, ticks or stat modifiers.
Every early end (`ArrivalProtection.End`) removes the held buff through the aura system that owns the character's
current roster (`AuraRoster.System`), preserving normal removal hooks and stat updates; movement ends it once the new
position is written. `ArrivalProtection.Is` is the one check of its id (the cap, the cache, save and restore). It has
one reserved slot outside
`Game:MaxAurasPerUnit`, so a full normal buff list neither prevents protection nor loses a buff. If a catalog edit
allows independent copies, only the first reserves that extra slot and movement ends every copy. A cached reference
avoids scanning buffs during combat/movement checks, and the character's clock enforces the exact deadline even before
the aura update pass. Leaving removes it. Saving and restoring exclude it, so it cannot carry over between sessions
or characters. Normal forward-only reload applies: a held copy keeps its definition until reapplied, a new application
uses the current catalog, and removing or refusing the row ends held copies on the next aura pass.

- **Data** (World DB reference data and balance tables, migrations `AddAuras` and `SeedAuraContent`): `AuraTemplates` (`Id`, `Name`, `Icon`, `Kind` Helpful 1 / Harmful 2, `DurationMs` above 0, `TickIntervalMs` at most the duration and 0 for an aura that never ticks, `PeriodicKind` None 0 / Damage 1 / Heal 2, `PeriodicBase`, `ScalingStat`, `ScalingCoefficient`, `BaseDamageCoefficient`, `Stacking` Refresh 1 / Stack 2 / Independent 3, `MaxStacks`, nullable `ScriptName`, and since #770 (migration `SeedCrowdControl`) `Control` (flags: Stun 1, Root 2, Silence 4; 0 for none) and the break chances `BreakOnDamagePct` and `BreakPerSecondPct`, percentages from 0 to 100) and `AuraStatModifiers` (keyed `(AuraId, Stat)`, cascading from the aura; `Stat` Armor 1, AttackDamage 2, AbilityDamage 3, CritPct 4, DodgePct 5, BlockPct 6, HastePct 7, MovementSpeed 8, MaxHealth 9, MaxPower 10; `Kind` Flat 1 / Percent 2; `Value`). The domain enums start at 1, so a missing value is refused rather than read as Helpful or Refresh (`AuraPeriodicKind.None` is 0). Check constraints hold the database to the rules: `CK_AuraTemplates_Kind`, `_PeriodicKind`, `_Stacking`, `_ScalingStat`, `_DurationMs`, `_TickIntervalMs` (at most the duration, above 0 for a periodic aura), `_MaxStacks` (1 or more), `_PeriodicBase`, `_ScalingCoefficient` and `_BaseDamageCoefficient` (finite, 0 or more), `_PeriodicFitsKind` (a harmful aura never heals, a helpful one never damages), `_Control` (0 to 7), `_ControlIsHarmful` (only a harmful aura stuns, roots or silences), `_BreakOnDamagePct` and `_BreakPerSecondPct` (0 to 100); `CK_AuraStatModifiers_Stat`, `_Kind`, `_Value` (finite, above -1,000,000, and a percentage above -100). `AbilityTemplate.AuraId` (nullable; foreign key `FK_AbilityTemplates_AuraTemplates_AuraId`, `Restrict`) names the aura every unit the ability's shape affects receives from the caster; the world reads it from the live ability catalog at apply time, never from `AbilityMetadata` (World.Public). An ability's direct amount is gated on its `Effects`: a Hostile ability hits only when `Effects` has `Damage`, an Ally one heals only with `Heal` (`AbilityRules.HasDirectEffect`), so an aura-only ability deals nothing directly and advertises `AbilityAmountKind.None`; `AbilityRules.Problem` refuses an ability with neither a direct amount nor an aura ("the ability does nothing").
- **Catalog.** `AuraCatalog` (`Avalon.World/Auras`; the Auras reload area, `StaticData.Auras`, empty before the first load) loads every row `AuraRules.Problem` accepts and whose `ScriptName`, when set, is a loaded `AuraScript`, and refuses the rest with an Error naming each: an unknown enum, a duration of 0, a periodic aura with no interval, an interval longer than the duration, `MaxStacks` below 1, a negative or non-finite amount, a harmful aura that heals or a helpful one that damages, an unknown stat or modifier kind, a non-finite modifier, a percentage at or below -100, a stat modified twice, an unknown control flag, a helpful aura that stuns, roots or silences, a break chance that is not a finite percentage from 0 to 100. The Abilities reload area checks its links against an aura catalog of its own read, built silently, so each aura refusal is logged once, by the Auras area. `AuraRules.LinkProblem` (the world's `AbilityCatalog`, the API and the simulator all call it) refuses an ability naming an aura that is missing or refused, or one that does not fit what it affects: a Hostile ability applies only a harmful aura, an Ally one only a helpful aura (`AuraRules.Fits`).
- **The rules in `Avalon.Combat`**, shared with the balance simulator: `AuraRules` (`Problem`, `LinkProblem`, `Fits`, `TickCount`, `Snapshot`, `TakeTick`, `NextStacks`, `KeysByCaster`, `IsSlow`), `CrowdControlRules` (#770: `ResistPct`, `Scale`, `DiminishPct`, `Breaks`), `AuraSchedule`, `AuraSnapshot`, `AuraStats`, `AuraStatTotals`, and `HitResolver.ResolvePeriodic`/`ResolvePeriodicHeal`. **The schedule is anchored at expiry:** `n = max(1, floor(DurationMs / TickIntervalMs))` ticks (none with no interval) at `ExpiresAt - j x Interval`, j from n-1 down to 0, so the last lands at expiry; `Due(now)` owes every tick whose time has come, never more than are left. **The snapshot**, taken once at apply from the caster (`HitResolver.AbilityBase`): `PeriodicBase + ScalingCoefficient x the caster's stat + BaseDamageCoefficient x one roll of its base damage` (a creature's natural `DamageMin..DamageMax`, a character's main hand, none without a weapon; drawn through the combat service's `ICombatRandom` only when the coefficient and the range are above 0), divided by n, kept with the caster's crit chance and level. A tick deals the per-tick amount times the stacks, rolls crit with that chance (the one draw) and, for damage, meets the target's armour against that level; never a dodge or a block. **The fraction carry:** each tick's unfloored amount goes into the aura's own `PeriodicCarry` and only whole points are dealt or healed (`AuraRules.TakeTick`, as regeneration takes them, no minimum of 1); the last tick rounds what is left, a half up, so the ticks add up to the total; a refresh starts the carry over. **Stacking:** Refresh renews the duration, schedule and snapshot (a fresh roll); Stack adds one up to `MaxStacks` and renews the same way; Independent keeps one copy per caster, each renewed by its own caster only. **The fold** (`AuraStats`): each stat becomes `(base + flat) x max(0, 1 + percent / 100)`, every modifier times the stacks held, a whole-number stat rounded and never below 0 (maximum health never below 1); the caps apply afterwards, where they already did. `MovementSpeed` and `HastePct` are percentage points: a Flat modifier adds points (Crippled's -30 is a 30 % slow), a Percent one scales the points the unit already has.
- **Holders.** `UnitAuras` (`Avalon.World/Auras`) is `CharacterEntity.Auras` and `Creature.Auras`, World-side, never on `ICharacter`, `ICreature` or World.Public, so no mod can add, end or read auras; any other `IUnit` holds none (`AuraHolders`). Each held copy is an `ActiveAura`: the template it was applied with (kept, so a reload never changes it), caster guid, source ability (`AuraSource`: its threat multiplier, heal threat and power gain), stacks, snapshot, schedule and carry, with a key unique among its unit's auras, from 1, the client's handle. A unit holds at most `Game:MaxAurasPerUnit` (default 32, range 1-256); past it a new aura is refused and logged, while a copy already held still refreshes or stacks. `UnitAuras.Control` caches the crowd control its auras grant together (see Crowd control).
- **`AuraSystem`** (one per `MapInstance`, `MapInstance.Auras`, reached through the World-side `IAuraHost`; tick thread only). **Apply** (`Apply(caster, target, aura, source)`): who may receive it is the caller's to decide (the ability's shape and `Hostility`, an item's party rule). It refuses an aura no longer loaded, one that no longer fits the ability applying it (a reload of either side), a dead target, a harmful aura on a target every hit passes over (invulnerable, a corpse, a creature walking home, which resists crowd control instead: see Crowd control) and a new aura past the cap, each refusal logged at most once per aura per 10 s; otherwise it takes the snapshot, places the copy, refreshes the stats it modifies and runs the script's `OnApply` or `OnStack`. A crowd-control aura may land for less than its row's duration, or be resisted whole (see Crowd control). A harmful aura from a caster enters combat through `CombatService.EnterAuraCombat`, as a dodge does: the encounter forms (the caster at the initial threat seed), a creature is told `OnAttacked`, both sides are tagged in combat; further threat comes only from ticks. **The pass** (`Update`) runs right after the ability cast system and before the item cast bars, the combat service and the creature scripts, on the instance's `TimeProvider`, on absolute times, so a late pass catches up every owed tick, aura by aura in the order applied. For each unit holding an aura as the pass starts (characters, then creatures): a dead one loses them all; a creature walking home loses its harmful ones (reason `Reset`, owner decision); then each aura expires if its template is no longer loaded (reason `Expired`), takes its owed ticks, ends if its script asked, breaks by chance once per whole second held when its row gives it a chance to (reason `Broken`, see Crowd control), and expires once its time is up, after its last tick. A tick that kills its unit ends every aura it holds. The caster is credited (threat, power gain, the kill) only while it is alive in that instance (`CasterHere`); otherwise the aura ticks on its snapshot from nobody, and a killing tick is `CreatureKilled(creature, null)`: nobody is eligible, so no experience or quest credit, and every table drop is free for all at once. **The empty-instance skip** (owner decision): an instance with no players runs no aura pass, and the first tick with players (or an arrival, before it joins) calls `SkipOwed`, which ends every aura whose end has passed and leaves the rest owing only the ticks (and the rolls to break, #770) at or after now, so nothing bursts on re-entry. **The roster** (`AuraRoster`, `AuraSystem.Roster`, #898): which units of the instance hold an aura and which changed since the last broadcast, kept as auras change rather than found by walking every character and creature each tick, so the pass, the broadcast's updates and their clearing cost nothing per unit that holds or changed nothing. A unit is bound to its instance's roster while it is a member: `MapInstance.AddCharacter` and `AddCreature` join it (listed at once if it arrives holding auras or carrying changes, as a character does from select or another instance), `RemoveCharacter` and `RemoveCreature` take it off both lists (a logout, a despawn, Change Character, a transfer, a corpse's removal and a hot reload's re-add all go through them). Its `UnitAuras` tells the roster of every change it records (`Add`, `Changed`, `Remove`, `Load`), so anything that applies or ends an aura through `UnitAuras` keeps it with nothing to add. A unit whose last aura ended is dropped at the next pass's start; the pass walks a copy taken then, so a unit that gains its first aura during the pass waits for the next one, as before. Characters and creatures are listed apart, so both the pass and the updates still take characters first; within each, units come in the order they became holders (or first changed that tick), no longer in the instance dictionaries' slot order (#898). **Death** ends every aura through `ICombatOutcomes.UnitDied`, which every death in `CombatService` reports, with the pass as a safety net. **Containment:** a tick that throws is logged (throttled per aura) and keeps its carry; anything else in a unit's pass is logged (throttled per unit) and the pass goes on; `MapInstance` contains the pass, an ability's application, the death removal and the breaks on damage (#770), each with its own `ThrottledErrorLog`.
- **Ticks** go through two World-side `CombatService` methods. `ApplyPeriodicDamage`: a target every hit passes over, or a dead character, takes nothing and draws nothing. With a character caster present the encounter forms, a creature gains threat (the dealt amount x the source ability's `ThreatMultiplier` x the class modifier) and hears `OnAttacked`, the caster gains the source ability's `PowerGainPerHit` per tick that damaged a living unit, and both are tagged in combat; a tick between two players restarts their PvP timers as a hit does, except in a town. A creature caster's tick counts only in the encounter its target is already in and never forms or rejoins one (the aura formed it when it landed), so a creature that gave up the fight and reset at home stays out of it, ticks or not. A creature's health is changed directly, and written only by a tick that dealt something (no `OnHit`, so its script broadcasts nothing), a character's through `OnPeriodicHit`, with Fury from damage taken; a god-mode character takes 0. `ApplyPeriodicHeal`: never the dead, never past the maximum (`HealRules.After`), heal threat by the source ability's `HealThreatPerHp` for the health actually restored. A damage tick of at least a whole point, or a heal tick that restored health, is reported through `ICombatOutcomes.PeriodicTick`, which sends the existing damage and heal packets with `AuraId` and no `AbilityId` to whoever hears a hit or a heal there (#532); a 0-point tick sends nothing but still counts as combat.
- **Abilities** apply theirs in `AbilityEffect.Apply`, after the direct amount: a Hostile ability with `Damage` hits first, and only a hit that landed (`HitOutcome.Landed`) applies the aura, so a dodged or ignored hit applies none, while an aura-only cast is never dodged; an Ally ability heals first when it has `Heal`, then applies its aura to every ally its circle covers, the caster included. `IAbilityArena.ApplyAbilityAura` reads the ability's live row, so an `AuraId` whose aura a reload dropped applies nothing (logged, throttled).
- **Stats.** A character folds its auras through `CharacterStatsCalculator.Calculate(..., character.Auras.StatTotals)` at every refresh, after gear, so select, gear, level-up and auras agree; an applied, stacked or removed aura with modifiers refreshes at once with `KeepShare` (`AuraStatsRefresh`), the saved `CharacterStats` row included, and the sheet, movement speed and ability amounts reach the client through their usual flushers. A creature folds `Creature.AuraTotals` into `Combat` (attack, ability damage, crit), `Defence` (armour, dodge, block), its haste (from 0) and its maximum health (on top of the party scaling, keeping its share); its slow is `Creature.SpeedFactor` (`1 + aura movement points / 100`, the points within the floor and cap it spawned with), read by both locomotions through `CreatureSpeed.Of`, so scripts keep writing `Speed` from their template.
- **Scripts.** `AuraScript` (`Avalon.World/Auras`, World-side, never on World.Public) is named by `AuraTemplate.ScriptName`, found by type name once, at `Load()` (never hot-reloaded), and built once per type through `QuestScriptServices` (`AuraScripts`, a DI singleton); one instance serves every aura that names it, so it keeps no per-aura state. Hooks: `OnApply` (a new copy), `OnTick` (after the tick's amount; not called when that tick ended its own aura, by killing its unit or breaking it, #770, since the script has then already heard `OnRemove`), `OnStack`, `OnRemove(ctx, reason)` (`AuraRemoveReason`: `Expired` 1, `Death` 2, `Cancelled` 3, `Script` 4, `Reset` 5, `Broken` 6, #770). Each is contained: a throw is logged at most once per aura and hook per 10 s, and the aura goes on. Each hook is handed a context of its own, the one object a hook allocates (#883): it is ended when the hook returns, so it is never handed out twice. `IAuraContext` gives the aura's reads (id, name, the target and its health, the caster, `CasterPresent`, stacks, time left, duration) and four acts that go through the owning services and work only while the hook runs: `Remove()` (reason `Script`, once the hook returns), `Damage(amount)` (a tick-like hit, crit and armour from the snapshot, the caster credited while present), `Heal(amount)` (a fixed heal) and `Tell(line)`. Aura scripts are trusted (owner decision): `Damage` does no hostility check, from any aura, a helpful one included. A first hook that kills its target ends every aura on it, and the corpse enters no combat. No aura script is shipped or seeded yet.
- **Saving.** `CharacterAuras` (Character DB, migration `AddCharacterAuras`, key `(CharacterId, Slot)`, cascading from `Characters`; `CK_CharacterAuras_Stacks` 1 or more, `CK_CharacterAuras_TicksLeft` 0 or more): aura id, caster guid, source ability, stacks, time left, duration, ticks owed, the snapshot (per tick and stack, crit, caster level), the carried fraction and when it was applied. Every change marks `SaveStateTracker.AurasChanged`, and every save of a character holding an aura, or marked by a change, carries a `CharacterAuraWrite` (built in `CharacterSaveSnapshot`) that deletes all its rows and inserts the current ones, the time left read on `CharacterEntity.Clock`; a dead character saves none. Only a character caster is saved: any other guid is numbered per run, so it is saved as nobody. Select restores them with `AuraRestore`, one more `CharacterSelectHandler.Step` and the last before the pending spawn, contained (a failure costs the auras, never the select): each under the template loaded now (a missing one dropped with a warning), at most `MaxAurasPerUnit` (the earliest applied kept; any row dropped marks the auras changed, so the next save deletes it), stacks capped at the template's `MaxStacks`, the carry restored, and one overdue tick kept (`AuraSchedule.Restore`: a save taken after a tick came due and before the pass took it still owes that tick); a restored aura next rolls to break on its own on the next whole second of the time it had held when saved, so no roll it already took is owed again (#770). Their time stands still while the character is offline and is **held until spawn** (`UnitAuras.HeldSince`): `MapInstance.AddCharacter` resumes it (`ResumeHeld`: each schedule, and each next roll to break, moved on by the time it waited), so the loading screen costs nothing, and a save taken meanwhile reads the time left at the hold. **Aura time also pauses while a character moves between instances** (portal, scroll, respawn, party return): `World.TransferPlayer` holds its auras at the removal (`UnitAuras.Hold`, unless already held) and the target's `AddCharacter` resumes them, shifted by the time between, so no tick is lost and none is paid in a burst (the move is synchronous today, so the shift is about 0). A logout or despawn holds nothing. Creatures never save.
- **Replication**, in each instance's state broadcast after `SMSG_WORLD_STATE_ADD`: `SMSG_AURA_LIST` (0x310E: `UnitGuid` 1, `Entries` 2) for every unit newly in a client's view that holds an aura, and always for the client's own character when it is (re)added, empty too, so every entry and transfer replaces what the client held; `SMSG_AURA_UPDATE` (0x310D, the same fields), at most one per unit per tick with that tick's changes in order, to the unit and every client it is listed to (`CharacterCharacterGameState.Knows`; since #981 the views are decided once per broadcast period, so a unit coming into range hears nothing until it is listed, with its list, at the next decision), skipped for a unit listed that tick; each unit's update is encoded once per broadcast pass, by the first client that hears it, and the same packet goes to every other (`SharedAuraUpdates`, `OutboundPacket.Share`, #980: its bytes depend only on the unit), the pass giving up its own references when it ends, also when it throws; only the units the roster lists as changed are walked for each client (characters first); every unit's changes are cleared once the broadcast is done, seen or not, and also when it throws, again by walking only the roster's changed units. `AuraEntryDto`: `AuraId` 1, `InstanceKey` 2, `CasterGuid` 3, `Stacks` 4, `RemainingMs` 5 (rounded up), `DurationMs` 6, `Action` 7 (`AuraUpdateAction`: `Applied` 1, `Refreshed` 2, `Stacked` 3, `Removed` 4; absent in a list), `Control` 8 (#770: the crowd control the copy puts its holder in, bits 1 stun, 2 root, 4 silence, 8 slow, 0 none; worked out once per copy as it is applied or renewed, `ActiveAura.WireControl`, never per entry, and sent in lists and updates alike, so a client never needs its catalog for it). Tick packets carry `AuraId` in `SUnitDamagePacket` field 6, `SCharacterDamagePacket` field 7 and `SUnitHealedPacket` field 7.
- **The cancel.** `CMSG_AURA_CANCEL` (0x2104: `AuraId` 1, optional `InstanceKey` 2), with a `MapSessionFilter` entry, reaches `AuraCancelHandler`, which ends the sender's own helpful auras through its instance's `AuraSystem.Cancel`, checked in the order `Dead`, `NotFound` (no copy of the aura, or none with that key, matched as given), `NotCancellable` (harmful), then `Ok`: with a key only that copy ends, without one every copy of the aura (owner decision), reason `Cancelled`. Exactly one `SMSG_AURA_CANCEL_RESULT` (0x310F: `AuraId` 1, `Result` 2; `AuraCancelResult` `Ok` 1, `NotFound` 2, `NotCancellable` 3, `Dead` 4, `Unknown` 0 never sent), sent at once, so ahead of that tick's `SMSG_AURA_UPDATE`; a throw is logged and answered `NotFound`; a connection with no character gets nothing.
- **Items.** `IItemUseContext.ApplyAura(aura, member?)` puts a **helpful** aura on the user, or on one of `PartyMembersHere`, with the user as caster and no source ability; false, doing nothing, for a dead user, anyone else, an aura not loaded or not helpful, or one the aura system refused; contained (`ItemUseTools.AuraFailures`).
- **The REST API.** `GET /world/{worldId}/aura-template` (paged, page size at most 50) and `/{id}` (`AuraTemplateController`, `[WorldScoped]`, Player policy) return `AuraTemplateDto` with its modifiers, `control` (the flags as an integer mask), `breakOnDamagePct`, `breakPerSecondPct`, `version` and `editable`; `PUT /world/{worldId}/aura-template/{id}` edits it live (see Live template editing). `AbilityTemplateDto` and its edit carry `auraId`. A character's saved auras are `GET /world/{worldId}/character/{id}/auras` (`CharacterAurasDto`, by slot, each caster as `casterCharacterId`, a character id of that world or null for nobody; the owner or a game master, as the other character sub-resources; 404 for an unknown character), as the world last saved them (`ICharacterAuraRepository`, a required dependency). The world publishes its aura script names in its script catalog (`aura`).
- **The exporters.** `tools/Avalon.Exporter -- aura-catalog` writes `schema/auras/aura-catalog-v1.json` (id, name, icon, kind, duration, tick interval, periodic kind, stacking, `maxStacks`, `control` (the flags; never the slow bit, which only the wire adds), `breakOnDamagePct`, `breakPerSecondPct` and modifiers, enumerations as numbers listed in its `$comment`; no amounts), pinned against the seed by `AuraCatalogExportShould`; `ability-catalog` carries each ability's `auraId`, and `basic` for a class's basic attack (`AbilityFlags.Basic`), the one ability a silence does not stop. Both need a World database.
- **The balance simulator** reads `AuraTemplates` and `AuraStatModifiers` from the seed, takes overrides keyed `Aura.<id>` and `AuraStatModifier.<auraId>.<Stat>`, and models damage and healing over time and stat auras through the same `Avalon.Combat` rules (snapshot, schedule, carry, fold, `ResolvePeriodic`); movement modifiers change nothing there (nobody moves), crowd control is never modelled (owner decision, #770: the new aura, rarity and creature columns are read and validated, and nothing stuns, roots, silences, resists, diminishes or breaks), the cap is never reached, no aura script runs and nothing stalls. `SimulatorParityShould` pins the aura cases against the server.

## Crowd control (#770)

Crowd control is data on the aura row, never a case per aura id. A harmful aura can **stun** (no moving, turning or
acting: no cast, item, doodad, gear change, loot pickup, NPC or portal; cancelling its own helpful auras, Bag moves and
chat still go), **root** (no moving; it still turns, casts and acts within reach) or **silence** (no ability but the
basic attack; it still moves and uses items and doodads). A **slow** is not a flag: it is any harmful aura with a
negative `MovementSpeed` modifier (`AuraRules.IsSlow`), so Crippled and Hexed are slows with no data change, and it
lowers the speed through the stat fold as before. A unit holding several is in every state any of them grants.

- **The cache and the gates.** `UnitAuras.Control` (World-side, internal) is the union of the held auras' `Control`
  flags, worked out again in `Record` whenever the changed aura or the current state has any (so a refresh onto a
  reloaded row that dropped its flags clears them) and in `Load`. `ControlGates` (`Avalon.World/Auras`) reads it with no
  scan, no clock and no allocation: `Of`, `CanMove` (not stunned or rooted), `CanTurn` (not stunned), `CanAct` (not
  stunned: items, doodads, gear, loot, NPCs, portals), `CanCast(unit, basic)` (not stunned, and not silenced unless the
  cast is the basic attack) and `IsBasic` (a row flagged `AbilityFlags.Basic`, or a creature's own basic). A unit that
  holds no auras is free. A state lasts until the aura pass removes its aura, every tick. The slow is never cached on
  the unit: only each copy's wire bit is.
- **Landing** (`AuraSystem.Apply`). On a **creature**, a crowd-control aura (one that stuns, roots or silences, or a
  slow) lands for `floor(duration x pct / 100)` (`CrowdControlRules.Scale`), the percentage being the smallest, among
  the types it grants, of the creature's `ControlResistance`. That is fixed at spawn (`CreatureSpawner`): the template's
  `StunDurationPct`, `RootDurationPct`, `SilenceDurationPct` or `SlowDurationPct` where set, else its rarity's
  `CreatureRarityModifiers` row, else 100; a `/reload creatures` reaches only creatures spawned after it. Slows now
  scale too: Crippled lasts 3 s on a Rare or a Boss. On a **character**, a stun, root or silence from another character
  takes its target's PvP diminishing step (`ControlDiminishing`, `CharacterEntity.Diminishing`, in memory only, never
  saved, so a new session starts clean): counted per type on the target, whoever cast it, the first lands at 100 %, the
  second at 50 %, the third at 25 % and the fourth is refused (`CrowdControlRules.DiminishPct`), and the count starts
  over once none of that type has landed for `Game:CrowdControlDiminishWindow` (default 6 s, above 0 and at most 7 s;
  [configuration](configuration-reference.md)). An aura granting several types takes the smallest step and records each.
  A creature's control on a character, an aura from nobody, and a slow alone never diminish. Every step that lands, a
  refresh included, is recorded and restarts the window; the refused step records nothing, so repeated tries cannot hold
  a target immune. **Nothing landing** (a resistance of 0, the refused step, or a creature walking home) is
  `AuraApplyResult.Resisted`: nothing is placed or recorded and the aura enters no combat; for an ability's aura its
  caster and whoever would have seen the hit get a hit of 0 marked `HitResult.Immune`
  (`ICombatOutcomes.ControlResisted`, sent as a dodge is), after the ability's own damage. A creature walking home is
  told so only by an ability with no direct amount: one with a direct amount, as all four seeded ones have, sees its hit
  passed over first, and a hit that did not land applies no aura, so nothing is resisted and nothing is sent. Arrival
  protection, an invulnerable creature and a corpse still refuse it (`Refused`, no Immune). A doodad's or an item's
  application that is resisted counts as not applied. A refresh of a held crowd-control aura never shortens it: the copy
  keeps the later of its expiry and the new one, its `DurationMs` then being the time it had left.
- **Interrupts.** A stun or a silence that landed (and that a first hook did not end at once) ends the target's running
  cast bar through its instance (`AuraSystem`'s `controlled` callback, `MapInstance.Controlled`): a stun ends its
  ability cast (`IAbilityCastSystem.Interrupt`) and, for a character, its item and doodad cast bars; a silence ends its
  ability cast unless that is the basic attack or a cast an item started (`AbilityInstance.ForItem`), and no item or
  doodad bar. Only the wind-up ends, out loud, with the usual interrupt and its cast id: nothing fires, nothing is
  refunded and no cooldown starts, while a hit already fired and held for the dodge wait still lands and a projectile in
  flight flies on (unlike `CancelCasts`). It is safe from inside a cast firing on that tick (a stun dealt by a cast). A
  root interrupts nothing; for a character moving still does, and a rooted character does not move.
- **Characters.** Input while stunned or rooted (`PlayerInputHandler`) is taken but no step is: its sequence advances
  and the ack carries the unchanged position and zero velocity, so the client reconciles without drifting, and its
  velocity is set to zero; a rooted character turns to the input's yaw, a stunned one keeps its own.
  `CastAbilityHandler` refuses `CastRejectReason.Stunned` (15) right after `Dead`, and `Silenced` (16) once the ability
  is known, after `NotOwned`; the cast system refuses the same on both paths (`QueueAbility`, `RunInstant`), the one
  choke point for players, creatures and items, a cast an item started being spared by a silence. A stun refuses, each
  reply with an appended `Stunned` reason: an item use, gear included (`ItemUseResult.Stunned` 17, right after `Dead`),
  a drag or a destroy that touches Equipment or the Bank (`ItemRequestResult.Stunned` 13; Bag moves and destroying a Bag
  item still go), vendor buy, sell and buyback (`VendorResult.Stunned` 13, after `Dead`, before the leash), quest accept
  and turn-in (`QuestResult.Stunned` 11), a ground-loot pickup (`LootPickupResult.Stunned` 7) and a portal entry
  (`MapTransitionResult.Stunned` 14, asked at the request and again on arrival, after the instance is built). An open
  NPC conversation stays open. NPC and doodad interaction (`InteractHandler`; `DoodadUseService` as
  `DoodadUseRefusal.Stunned`) and dialogue choices are dropped silently, as their other refusals are. Cancelling one's
  own helpful aura (`CMSG_AURA_CANCEL`) is allowed. God mode does not lift crowd control.
- **Creatures.** `CreatureCombatScript` reads the cache once a tick as it engages: stunned, it stands as for a wind-up
  (a walk in progress stopped, no turn, no attack, no cast); rooted, it asks for no step and claims no new melee slot,
  but turns to and attacks a target in reach; silenced, it casts only its basic, ready and in reach (`ChooseAbility`,
  and so a forest rotation, is not asked: a rotation preferring a ready special would leave it attacking nothing).
  Neither stun nor root counts toward the 5 s unreachable give-up; the leash and the walk home are unchanged.
  `CreaturePatrolScript` takes no step while stunned or rooted: a leg in progress is stopped and asked for again once it
  is free, so its point is neither skipped nor counted reached. A slow needs nothing: the locomotion moves the creature
  at its slowed speed. The creature scheduler needs no hold: a fighting or walking creature is updated every tick, and a
  parked one reads its state when it wakes. Known limits: a rooted creature keeps the melee slot it already held for the
  root's few seconds; a crowd's separation can still push a controlled creature; a patrol's pause at a point runs on
  through crowd control.
- **Breaks** (`AuraRemoveReason.Broken` 6, World-side; a client hears an ordinary `Removed`). Two chances per aura row,
  in percent. `BreakOnDamagePct` is rolled once by each aura its holder holds whenever a hit or a periodic tick deals
  more than 0 to it and leaves it alive (`CombatService` reports `ICombatOutcomes.DamageTaken`, once per hit or tick, to
  `AuraSystem.DamageTaken`; the copies that break are ended after the walk). `BreakPerSecondPct` is rolled by the aura
  pass once for every whole second held (`ActiveAura.NextBreakRollAt`: a second after the copy was applied or renewed,
  then every second), never at or past its end, after its ticks and before its expiry. A late pass rolls every second it
  owes, as it pays ticks; the empty-instance skip, a hold (the loading screen, a move between instances) and a restore
  at select do not: the next roll keeps its place on the whole seconds held, so no burst of owed rolls follows. Every
  roll draws from the combat service's `ICombatRandom`, and a chance of 0 draws nothing. A hit on a unit holding no aura
  with a `BreakOnDamagePct` walks none of its auras: `UnitAuras.BreaksOnDamage` caches whether any held aura has one,
  worked out again by the same rule as `Control` (in `Record` when the changed aura or the current state has one, and in
  `Load`). Any aura row may carry the two chances; the seed gives them only to the crowd-control auras and the two slows.
- **Data.** World migration `SeedCrowdControl`: the aura columns above; on `CreatureRarityModifiers` four duration
  percentages, `StunDurationPct`, `RootDurationPct`, `SilenceDurationPct` and `SlowDurationPct` (0 immune to 100 the
  full duration, default 100; seeded Normal and Elite 100 each, Rare 50 each, Boss 0 stun, 50 root, 0 silence, 50 slow);
  the same four on `CreatureTemplates`, nullable (null takes the rarity's; none seeded). Check constraints
  `CK_CreatureRarityModifiers_<Type>DurationPct` and `CK_CreatureTemplates_<Type>DurationPct`, and
  `CreatureTemplateRules.Validate` and `ValidateRarities` (the Creatures reload area, the balance simulator, the API's
  edit), refuse anything outside 0 to 100. All are balance tables. The REST API edits the aura columns and the creature
  overrides ([live template editing](live-template-editing.md)); the rarity table has no REST surface. The client
  contract is [Aura protocol](aura-protocol.md), section 10.
