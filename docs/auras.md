# Auras (server design)

Server-side rules for auras. The client contract is [Aura protocol](aura-protocol.md).

## Auras

Auras: timed effects on a character or a creature, periodic damage or healing, stat modifiers (buffs, debuffs, slows), or both, put on by abilities, item scripts, aura scripts and the Resonance effects of worn gear ([Resonance effects](#resonance-effects-1180)). A harmful aura can also put its holder under crowd control: a stun, a root, a silence or a slow ([Crowd control](#crowd-control-770)). No dispel yet. `docs/aura-protocol.md` is the client contract.

### Arrival protection (#1088)

Every living character receives **Arrival Protection** (reserved aura 11) on world entry and map arrival,
a helpful buff lasting **60 seconds**. Login and reconnect keep the existing readiness barrier; the protection starts
when the character actually enters the instance. Portals and respawns grant a fresh copy. The usual aura list/update
packets display its countdown, and the player can cancel it through `CMSG_AURA_CANCEL`.

The protection prevents enemy targeting, creature aggro, direct and periodic damage, and new harmful auras. Helpful
area effects can still affect the character. It protects the holder only as a target: a protected character cannot
act without ending it, and an aura it put on others before it arrived still ticks, threatens and tags combat as usual.
The buff ends when its character changes horizontal position, when the cast system takes a cast of its (cast bar or
instant, item casts included: `InstanceAbilityCastSystem.QueueAbility`/`RunInstant`, before the start is sent or the
cast fires), when an item use of its passes its checks (a script use, a cast bar, an equip or unequip: `ItemUseService`,
with the item and doodad cast interrupts), when a drag of its equips or unequips gear (`ItemMoveHandler`, an accepted
`CMSG_ITEM_MOVE` touching Equipment), when a doodad use of its passes its checks (instant or cast bar:
`DoodadUseService.Interact`, with the same interrupts), when its 60 seconds expire, or when its owner cancels it. A
refused cast, use, drag or interaction keeps it, and so does a drag that touches no gear. Turning, idle input and pressing into a wall without moving do not end it. A portal or teleport removes
the old protection when leaving or repositioning and grants fresh protection on arrival.

The buff is seeded with the other auras and loaded through `StaticData.Auras`. Arrival applies its ID through the
destination instance's `AuraSystem`, using the loaded duration and metadata; a missing or refused row grants no
protection and uses the ordinary throttled refusal log. The seeded row has no script, ticks or stat modifiers.
Every early end (`ArrivalProtection.End`) removes the held buff through the aura system that owns the character's
current roster (`AuraRoster.System`), preserving normal removal hooks and stat updates; movement ends it once the new
position is written. `ArrivalProtection.Is` is the one check of its id (the cap, the cache, save and restore). It has
one reserved slot outside
`Game:MaxAurasPerUnit`, so a full normal buff list neither prevents protection nor loses a buff. If a catalog edit
allows independent copies, only the first reserves that extra slot and movement ends every copy. A cached reference
avoids scanning buffs during combat/movement checks, and the character's clock enforces the exact deadline even before
the aura update pass. Leaving removes it. Saving and restoring exclude it, so it cannot carry over between sessions
or characters. Normal forward-only reload applies: a held copy keeps its definition until reapplied, a new application
uses the current catalog, and removing or refusing the row ends held copies on the next aura pass.

- **Data** (World DB reference data and balance tables, migrations `AddAuras` and `SeedAuraContent`): `AuraTemplates` (`Id`, `Name`, `Icon`, `Kind` Helpful 1 / Harmful 2, `DurationMs` above 0, `TickIntervalMs` at most the duration and 0 for an aura that never ticks, `PeriodicKind` None 0 / Damage 1 / Heal 2, `PeriodicBase` and, since #1178, `PeriodicEffectiveness` (the percent of its source's base roll it adds), `Stacking` Refresh 1 / Stack 2 / Independent 3, `MaxStacks`, nullable `ScriptName`, and since #770 (migration `SeedCrowdControl`) `Control` (flags: Stun 1, Root 2, Silence 4; 0 for none) and the break chances `BreakOnDamagePct` and `BreakPerSecondPct`, percentages from 0 to 100) and `AuraStatModifiers` (keyed `(AuraId, Stat)`, cascading from the aura; `Stat` Armor 1, CritPct 4, Evasion 5 (a rating, #1178), BlockPct 6, MovementSpeed 8, MaxHealth 9, MaxPower 10, and since #1178 FireResistance 11, ColdResistance 12, LightningResistance 13, BlightResistance 14, MaxWard 15, the thirteen increased percentages IncreasedDamage 16, IncreasedPhysicalDamage 17, IncreasedFireDamage 18, IncreasedColdDamage 19, IncreasedLightningDamage 20, IncreasedBlightDamage 21, IncreasedAttackDamage 22, IncreasedSpellDamage 23, IncreasedAreaDamage 24, IncreasedProjectileDamage 25, IncreasedMeleeDamage 26, IncreasedDamageOverTime 27 and IncreasedHealing 28, CritMultiplier 29 (points over the formula's multiplier: 50 makes a 1.5 crit 2.0), AttackSpeed 30, CastSpeed 31 and CooldownRecovery 32 (2, 3 and 7, the old damage stats and haste, are retired and never reused); `Kind` Flat 1 / Increased 2 / More 3; `Value`). The domain enums start at 1, so a missing value is refused rather than read as Helpful or Refresh (`AuraPeriodicKind.None` is 0). Check constraints hold the database to the rules: `CK_AuraTemplates_Kind`, `_PeriodicKind`, `_Stacking`, `_DurationMs`, `_TickIntervalMs` (at most the duration, above 0 for a periodic aura), `_MaxStacks` (1 or more), `_PeriodicBase` and `_PeriodicEffectiveness` (finite, 0 or more), `_PeriodicFitsKind` (a harmful aura never heals, a helpful one never damages), `_Control` (0 to 7), `_ControlIsHarmful` (only a harmful aura stuns, roots or silences), `_BreakOnDamagePct` and `_BreakPerSecondPct` (0 to 100); `CK_AuraStatModifiers_Stat` (1 to 32 but 2, 3 and 7), `_Kind` (1 to 3), `_Value` (finite, above -1,000,000, and an Increased or a More above -100). `AbilityTemplate.AuraId` (nullable; foreign key `FK_AbilityTemplates_AuraTemplates_AuraId`, `Restrict`) names the aura every unit the ability's shape affects receives from the caster; the world reads it from the live ability catalog at apply time, never from `AbilityMetadata` (World.Public). An ability's direct amount is gated on its `Effects`: a Hostile ability hits only when `Effects` has `Damage`, an Ally one heals only with `Heal` (`AbilityRules.HasDirectEffect`), so an aura-only ability deals nothing directly and advertises `AbilityAmountKind.None`; `AbilityRules.Problem` refuses an ability with neither a direct amount nor an aura ("the ability does nothing"), and `AbilityFlags.Unavoidable` on an Ally ability (#1164); `AuraRules.DamageTypeProblem` refuses a class ability (one the talent tree grants, #1181) whose aura deals damage over time and which names no damage type (#1178; Ignite names Fire), and the world's `AbilityCatalog`, the API's template edits and the simulator call it with `LinkProblem`.
- **Catalog.** `AuraCatalog` (`Avalon.World/Auras`; the Auras reload area, `StaticData.Auras`, empty before the first load) loads every row `AuraRules.Problem` accepts and whose `ScriptName`, when set, is a loaded `AuraScript`, and refuses the rest with an Error naming each: an unknown enum, a duration of 0, a periodic aura with no interval, an interval longer than the duration, `MaxStacks` below 1, a negative or non-finite amount, a harmful aura that heals or a helpful one that damages, an unknown stat or modifier kind, a non-finite modifier, an Increased or a More at or below -100, an Increased on one of the thirteen `Increased…` stats, `CritMultiplier` or the three speeds (`AttackSpeed`, `CastSpeed`, `CooldownRecovery`), percentages already (they take Flat, "+X %", or More, "X % more"), a stat modified twice, an unknown control flag, a helpful aura that stuns, roots or silences, a break chance that is not a finite percentage from 0 to 100. The Abilities reload area checks its links against an aura catalog of its own read, built silently, so each aura refusal is logged once, by the Auras area. `AuraRules.LinkProblem` (the world's `AbilityCatalog`, the API and the simulator all call it) refuses an ability naming an aura that is missing or refused, or one that does not fit what it affects: a Hostile ability applies only a harmful aura, an Ally one only a helpful aura (`AuraRules.Fits`). `AuraRules.SourceProblem` (#1178; the world's `AbilityCatalog` and the API's template edits call it) refuses an ability whose tags do not fit the aura it applies: a class ability (one the talent tree grants, #1181) applying a damaging aura is an Attack or a Spell, an Ally ability applying a healing aura a Heal, and a Spell or a Heal whose aura ticks has a spell base for the aura to draw on.
- **The rules in `Avalon.Combat`**, shared with the balance simulator: `AuraRules` (`Problem`, `LinkProblem`, `Fits`, `TickCount`, `Snapshot`, `TakeTick`, `NextStacks`, `KeysByCaster`, `IsSlow`), `CrowdControlRules` (#770: `ResistPct`, `Scale`, `DiminishPct`, `Breaks`), `AuraSchedule`, `AuraSnapshot`, `AuraStats`, `AuraStatTotals`, and `HitResolver.ResolvePeriodic`/`ResolvePeriodicHeal`. **The schedule is anchored at expiry:** `n = max(1, floor(DurationMs / TickIntervalMs))` ticks (none with no interval) at `ExpiresAt - j x Interval`, j from n-1 down to 0, so the last lands at expiry; `Due(now)` owes every tick whose time has come, never more than are left. **The snapshot**, taken once at apply from the caster and the source ability (`AuraRules.Snapshot` over `OffenceMath.PeriodicTotal`, #1178): the total is `(PeriodicBase + source roll x PeriodicEffectiveness / 100) x scale`. The source roll is the caster's weapon (both ranges: a character's main hand, or the formula's unarmed range with none; a creature's natural range) for an Attack, else the source's spell base grown by the caster's level, never the source's conversion or built-in added damage (those belong to its hit), drawn through the combat service's `ICombatRandom` only when `PeriodicEffectiveness` is above 0. A damage aura scales by `OffenceMath.Scale` of its damage type with its source's tags plus DamageOverTime (so increased damage over time counts, and Strength's physical attack bonus does for a bleed from an Attack), a heal aura by increased healing alone. With no source (an item's or a script's aura, or no caster), `PeriodicBase` alone. The total is fixed then: nothing the caster does afterwards (a weapon swap, dying, logging out) changes a tick. It is divided by n and kept with the caster's crit chance, level and crit multiplier bonus (#1178: a tick that crits multiplies by the formula's `CritMultiplier` plus it) and, for damage, the aura's damage type (#1178): the source ability's `DamageType`, else the caster's base type (a creature's template's), Physical with no caster. A tick deals the per-tick amount times the stacks, rolls crit with that chance (the one draw) and, for damage, meets the target's mitigation for its type: armour against that level for physical, the resistance for an element; never evaded or blocked. **The ward** (#1178) takes a damage tick before health, except a blight tick or a physical one (a bleed), which pass it (`HitResolver.WardAbsorbs(type, periodic: true)`); any damage tick, one that passed the ward included, pauses its recharge, and a heal tick never restores it. **The fraction carry:** each tick's unfloored amount goes into the aura's own `PeriodicCarry` and only whole points are dealt or healed (`AuraRules.TakeTick`, as regeneration takes them, no minimum of 1); the last tick rounds what is left, a half up, so the ticks add up to the total; a refresh starts the carry over. **Stacking:** Refresh renews the duration, schedule and snapshot (a fresh roll); Stack adds one up to `MaxStacks` and renews the same way; Independent keeps one copy per caster, each renewed by its own caster only. **The fold** (`AuraStats`): each stat becomes `(base + flat) x max(0, 1 + increased / 100) x each (1 + more / 100)` (#1178), a Flat or Increased modifier times the stacks held and a More multiplying once per stack; `AuraRules.Problem` refuses an Increased or a More at or below -100, and an Increased on the thirteen `Increased…` keys, `CritMultiplier` and the three speeds, which are percentages already (they take Flat, "+X %", or More); a whole-number stat rounded and never below 0 (maximum health never below 1); the caps apply afterwards, where they already did. `MovementSpeed`, the three speeds and `CritMultiplier` are percentage points and the thirteen `Increased…` stats percentages of increase: a Flat modifier adds points (Crippled's -30 is a 30 % slow, Emboldened's +10 is 10 % increased damage), and an Increased one scales the points the unit already has (on `MovementSpeed` alone: the speeds and `CritMultiplier` refuse it); on an `Increased…` stat a More is a separate multiplier of that damage or healing, kept apart from the increased sum (`OffenceStats`: "10 % more damage").
- **Holders.** `UnitAuras` (`Avalon.World/Auras`) is `CharacterEntity.Auras` and `Creature.Auras`, World-side, never on `ICharacter`, `ICreature` or World.Public, so no mod can add, end or read auras; any other `IUnit` holds none (`AuraHolders`). Each held copy is an `ActiveAura`: the template it was applied with (kept, so a reload never changes it), caster guid, source ability (`AuraSource`: its threat multiplier, heal threat and power gain), stacks, snapshot, schedule and carry, with a key unique among its unit's auras, from 1, the client's handle. **Copies are pooled (#1180 part A2):** each instance's `AuraSystem` keeps the copies it made itself (`ActiveAura.Pool`, an empty token object of its own that references nothing, compared once on removal), from any source of aura, in a list once it removes them until its next pass starts, then lets go of everything they reference (template, caster, source) and hands them to the next new auras placed there before it makes one, so a placement allocates nothing once the pool holds what the instance's units shed between two passes. A copy made elsewhere (by another instance and carried in, or restored at login) is left to the collector when it ends here, so each pool is bounded by its own instance's peak: a town that ends the buffs its visitors bring keeps none of them. A copy goes back once per removal (`AuraSystem.Remove`, the one way off a unit), and is placed again no sooner than the next pass: nothing that saw it removed (a pass's walk, a removal path's copy, a hook, the caller) still runs by then. Nothing keeps a copy past its removal: an update or a list carries values (`AuraChange`, `AuraEntryDto`), a save copies rows, and an `AuraContext` a script kept answers what the aura was as its hook ended, never the copy. A unit holds at most `Game:MaxAurasPerUnit` (default 32, range 1-256); past it a new aura is refused and logged, while a copy already held still refreshes or stacks. `UnitAuras.Control` caches the crowd control its auras grant together (see Crowd control).
- **`AuraSystem`** (one per `MapInstance`, `MapInstance.Auras`, reached through the World-side `IAuraHost`; tick thread only). **Apply** (`Apply(caster, target, aura, source)`): who may receive it is the caller's to decide (the ability's shape and `Hostility`, an item's party rule). It refuses an aura no longer loaded, one that no longer fits the ability applying it (a reload of either side), a dead target, a harmful aura on a target every hit passes over (invulnerable, a corpse, a creature walking home, which resists crowd control instead: see Crowd control) and a new aura past the cap, each refusal logged at most once per aura per 10 s; otherwise it takes the snapshot, places the copy, refreshes the stats it modifies and runs the script's `OnApply` or `OnStack`. A crowd-control aura may land for less than its row's duration, or be resisted whole (see Crowd control). A harmful aura from a caster enters combat through `CombatService.EnterAuraCombat`, as an evaded hit does: the encounter forms (a fight newly formed or joined holds the caster at the initial threat seed; one merged with the caster's seeds nothing, #1168), a creature is told `OnAttacked`, both sides are tagged in combat; further threat comes only from ticks. **The pass** (`Update`) runs right after the ability cast system and before the item cast bars, the combat service and the creature scripts, on the instance's `TimeProvider`, on absolute times, so a late pass catches up every owed tick, aura by aura in the order applied. For each unit holding an aura as the pass starts (characters, then creatures): a dead one loses them all; a creature walking home loses its harmful ones (reason `Reset`, owner decision); then each aura expires if its template is no longer loaded (reason `Expired`), takes its owed ticks, ends if its script asked, breaks by chance once per whole second held when its row gives it a chance to (reason `Broken`, see Crowd control), and expires once its time is up, after its last tick. A tick that kills its unit ends every aura it holds. The caster is credited (threat, power gain, the kill) only while it is alive in that instance (`CasterHere`); otherwise the aura ticks on its snapshot from nobody, and a killing tick is `CreatureKilled(creature, null)`: nobody is eligible, so no experience or quest credit, and every table drop is free for all at once. **The empty-instance skip** (owner decision): an instance with no players runs no aura pass, and the first tick with players (or an arrival, before it joins) calls `SkipOwed`, which ends every aura whose end has passed and leaves the rest owing only the ticks (and the rolls to break, #770) at or after now, so nothing bursts on re-entry. **The roster** (`AuraRoster`, `AuraSystem.Roster`, #898): which units of the instance hold an aura and which changed since the last broadcast, kept as auras change rather than found by walking every character and creature each tick, so the pass, the broadcast's updates and their clearing cost nothing per unit that holds or changed nothing. A unit is bound to its instance's roster while it is a member: `MapInstance.AddCharacter` and `AddCreature` join it (listed at once if it arrives holding auras or carrying changes, as a character does from select or another instance), `RemoveCharacter` and `RemoveCreature` take it off both lists (a logout, a despawn, Change Character, a transfer, a corpse's removal and a hot reload's re-add all go through them). Its `UnitAuras` tells the roster of every change it records (`Add`, `Changed`, `Remove`, `Load`), so anything that applies or ends an aura through `UnitAuras` keeps it with nothing to add. A unit whose last aura ended is dropped at the next pass's start; the pass walks a copy taken then, so a unit that gains its first aura during the pass waits for the next one, as before. Characters and creatures are listed apart, so both the pass and the updates still take characters first; within each, units come in the order they became holders (or first changed that tick), no longer in the instance dictionaries' slot order (#898). **Death** ends every aura through `ICombatOutcomes.UnitDied`, which every death in `CombatService` reports, with the pass as a safety net. **Containment:** a tick that throws is logged (throttled per aura) and keeps its carry; anything else in a unit's pass is logged (throttled per unit) and the pass goes on; `MapInstance` contains the pass, an ability's application, the death removal and the breaks on damage (#770), each with its own `ThrottledErrorLog`.
- **Ticks** go through two World-side `CombatService` methods. `ApplyPeriodicDamage`: a target every hit passes over, or a dead character, takes nothing and draws nothing. With a character caster present the encounter forms, a creature gains threat (the dealt amount x the source ability's `ThreatMultiplier` x the class modifier) and hears `OnAttacked`, and both are tagged in combat (no tick gives power, #1181); a tick between two players restarts their PvP timers as a hit does, except in a town. A creature caster's tick counts only in the encounter its target is already in and never forms or rejoins one (the aura formed it when it landed), so a creature that gave up the fight and reset at home stays out of it, ticks or not. A creature's health is changed directly, and written only by a tick that dealt something (no `OnHit`, so its script broadcasts nothing), a character's through `OnPeriodicHit`; a god-mode character takes 0. `ApplyPeriodicHeal`: never the dead, never past the maximum (`HealRules.After`), heal threat by the source ability's `HealThreatPerHp` for the health actually restored. A damage tick of at least a whole point, or a heal tick that restored health, is reported through `ICombatOutcomes.PeriodicTick`, which sends the existing damage and heal packets with `AuraId` and no `AbilityId` to whoever hears a hit or a heal there (#532); a 0-point tick sends nothing but still counts as combat.
- **Abilities** apply theirs in `AbilityEffect.Apply`, after the direct amount: every Hostile ability rolls whether it lands on each unit it affects (#1164, `IAbilityArena.HitForAbility`, `CombatService.ApplyAbilityHit`): the target's chance to evade it (`HitResolver.Evades`, its evasion rating against the caster's level, the same draw a damage hit makes first), the dodge wait for a telegraph's players (#1082), and the targets every hit passes over (`HitOutcome.Ignored`: an invulnerable creature, a corpse, a creature walking home). It then deals its damage only when it has `Damage`, and applies the aura only when it landed (`HitOutcome.Landed`), so an evaded or ignored hit applies none, with or without damage. One without `Damage` draws the evasion roll alone and counts as combat either way, as an evaded hit does, whether or not its aura then sticks (resisted as Immune, or refused): the encounter forms, a creature's script is told it was attacked, both are tagged in combat, with no threat beyond the encounter's and no PvP timer restarted (only damage dealt restarts one). Landed, it sends nothing and adds no crit, block or mitigation, and the aura's own combat entry (`EnterAuraCombat`) that follows changes nothing more; evaded, it is sent as a hit of 0 marked `Dodged`, naming the ability's type and no parts. An ability flagged `AbilityFlags.Unavoidable` is never rolled against: it draws no evasion roll, its damage is never evaded, and its aura is offered to every unit its shape affects, as an aura-only ability's was before #1164 (the aura system's own refusals, such as an invulnerable creature, a corpse or a creature walking home, still apply) (the catalog refuses the flag on an Ally ability; no seeded ability has it). Aura ticks are never evaded. An Ally ability heals first when it has `Heal`, then applies its aura to every ally its circle covers, the caster included. `IAbilityArena.ApplyAbilityAura` reads the ability's live row, so an `AuraId` whose aura a reload dropped applies nothing (logged, throttled).
- **Stats.** A character folds its auras through `CharacterStatsCalculator.Calculate(..., character.Auras.StatTotals)` at every refresh, after gear, so select, gear, level-up and auras agree; an applied, stacked or removed aura with modifiers refreshes at once with `KeepShare` (`AuraStatsRefresh`), the saved `CharacterStats` row included, and the sheet, movement speed and ability amounts reach the client through their usual flushers. A character's offence modifiers (the `Increased…` stats, `CritMultiplier` and the three speeds, #1178) fold once, inside `CharacterOffence.Of`, into its offence block and pace, never again in the fold of its other stats. A character's resistances, evasion and maximum ward fold the same way (#1178): a Flat resistance modifier adds points, so a negative one is how a debuff lowers a resistance, and an Increased one scales what the unit has, so an increased buff deepens a negative resistance; a resistance is not rounded and may fall below 0. A creature folds `Creature.AuraTotals` into `Combat` (crit, and its offence block, #1178: the `Increased…` stats and the crit multiplier, folded once in `Creature.ApplyAuraStats` when its auras change, never on a read), `Defence` (armour, evasion, block, the four resistances), its maximum ward (`Creature.ApplyAuraStats`, keeping its share; one grown from 0 starts empty and recharges), its attack speed, cast speed and cooldown recovery (from 0, #1178) and its maximum health (on top of the party scaling, keeping its share); its slow is `Creature.SpeedFactor` (`1 + aura movement points / 100`, the points within the floor and cap it spawned with), read by both locomotions through `CreatureSpeed.Of`, so scripts keep writing `Speed` from their template.
- **Scripts.** `AuraScript` (`Avalon.World/Auras`, World-side, never on World.Public) is named by `AuraTemplate.ScriptName`, found by type name once, at `Load()` (never hot-reloaded), and built once per type through `QuestScriptServices` (`AuraScripts`, a DI singleton); one instance serves every aura that names it, so it keeps no per-aura state. Hooks: `OnApply` (a new copy), `OnTick` (after the tick's amount; not called when that tick ended its own aura, by killing its unit or breaking it, #770, since the script has then already heard `OnRemove`), `OnStack`, `OnRemove(ctx, reason)` (`AuraRemoveReason`: `Expired` 1, `Death` 2, `Cancelled` 3, `Script` 4, `Reset` 5, `Broken` 6, #770). Each is contained: a throw is logged at most once per aura and hook per 10 s, and the aura goes on. Each hook is handed a context of its own, the one object a hook allocates (#883): it is ended when the hook returns, so it is never handed out twice. `IAuraContext` gives the aura's reads (id, name, the target and its health, the caster, `CasterPresent`, stacks, time left, duration) and four acts that go through the owning services and work only while the hook runs: `Remove()` (reason `Script`, once the hook returns), `Damage(amount)` (a tick-like hit, crit and the mitigation of the snapshot's type, the ward as a tick of that type meets it, the caster credited while present), `Heal(amount)` (a fixed heal) and `Tell(line)`. Aura scripts are trusted (owner decision): `Damage` does no hostility check, from any aura, a helpful one included. A first hook that kills its target ends every aura on it, and the corpse enters no combat. No aura script is shipped or seeded yet.
- **Saving.** `CharacterAuras` (Character DB, migration `AddCharacterAuras`, key `(CharacterId, Slot)`, cascading from `Characters`; `CK_CharacterAuras_Stacks` 1 or more, `CK_CharacterAuras_TicksLeft` 0 or more): aura id, caster guid, source ability, stacks, time left, duration, ticks owed, the snapshot (per tick and stack, crit, caster level, its damage type in `DamageType` and the caster's crit multiplier bonus in `CritMultiplierBonus`, #1178; Character migrations `AddDefenceStats` and `AddAuraCritBonus`, a row saved before either restoring as Physical with no bonus), the carried fraction and when it was applied. Every change marks `SaveStateTracker.AurasChanged`, and every save of a character holding an aura, or marked by a change, carries a `CharacterAuraWrite` (built in `CharacterSaveSnapshot`) that deletes all its rows and inserts the current ones, the time left read on `CharacterEntity.Clock`; a dead character saves none. Only a character caster is saved: any other guid is numbered per run, so it is saved as nobody. Select restores them with `AuraRestore`, one more `CharacterSelectHandler.Step` and the last before the pending spawn, contained (a failure costs the auras, never the select): each under the template loaded now (a missing one dropped with a warning), at most `MaxAurasPerUnit` (the earliest applied kept; any row dropped marks the auras changed, so the next save deletes it), stacks capped at the template's `MaxStacks`, the carry restored (and the damage type: a row saved before #1178, or one holding an unknown value, restores as Physical), and one overdue tick kept (`AuraSchedule.Restore`: a save taken after a tick came due and before the pass took it still owes that tick); a restored aura next rolls to break on its own on the next whole second of the time it had held when saved, so no roll it already took is owed again (#770). Their time stands still while the character is offline and is **held until spawn** (`UnitAuras.HeldSince`): `MapInstance.AddCharacter` resumes it (`ResumeHeld`: each schedule, and each next roll to break, moved on by the time it waited), so the loading screen costs nothing, and a save taken meanwhile reads the time left at the hold. **Aura time also pauses while a character moves between instances** (portal, scroll, respawn, party return): `World.TransferPlayer` holds its auras at the removal (`UnitAuras.Hold`, unless already held) and the target's `AddCharacter` resumes them, shifted by the time between, so no tick is lost and none is paid in a burst (the move is synchronous today, so the shift is about 0). A logout or despawn holds nothing. Creatures never save.
- **Replication**, in each instance's state broadcast after `SMSG_WORLD_STATE_ADD`: `SMSG_AURA_LIST` (0x310E: `UnitGuid` 1, `Entries` 2) for every unit newly in a client's view that holds an aura, and always for the client's own character when it is (re)added, empty too, so every entry and transfer replaces what the client held; `SMSG_AURA_UPDATE` (0x310D, the same fields), at most one per unit per tick with that tick's changes in order, to the unit and every client it is listed to (`CharacterCharacterGameState.Knows`; since #981 the views are decided once per broadcast period, so a unit coming into range hears nothing until it is listed, with its list, at the next decision), skipped for a unit listed that tick; each unit's update is encoded once per broadcast pass, by the first client that hears it, and the same packet goes to every other (`SharedAuraUpdates`, `OutboundPacket.Share`, #980: its bytes depend only on the unit), the pass giving up its own references when it ends, also when it throws; only the units the roster lists as changed are walked for each client (characters first); every unit's changes are cleared once the broadcast is done, seen or not, and also when it throws, again by walking only the roster's changed units. `AuraEntryDto`: `AuraId` 1, `InstanceKey` 2, `CasterGuid` 3, `Stacks` 4, `RemainingMs` 5 (rounded up), `DurationMs` 6, `Action` 7 (`AuraUpdateAction`: `Applied` 1, `Refreshed` 2, `Stacked` 3, `Removed` 4; absent in a list), `Control` 8 (#770: the crowd control the copy puts its holder in, bits 1 stun, 2 root, 4 silence, 8 slow, 0 none; worked out once per copy as it is applied or renewed, `ActiveAura.WireControl`, never per entry, and sent in lists and updates alike, so a client never needs its catalog for it). Tick packets carry `AuraId` in `SUnitDamagePacket` field 6, `SCharacterDamagePacket` field 7 and `SUnitHealedPacket` field 7.

## Crowd control (#770)

Crowd control is data on the aura row, never a case per aura id. A harmful aura can **stun** (no moving, turning or
acting: no cast, item, doodad, gear change, loot pickup, NPC or portal; cancelling its own helpful auras, Bag moves and
chat still go), **root** (no moving; it still turns, casts and acts within reach) or **silence** (no ability but the
basic attack; it still moves and uses items and doodads). A **slow** is not a flag: it is any harmful aura with a
negative `MovementSpeed` modifier (`AuraRules.IsSlow`), so Crippled and Hexed are slows with no data change, and it
lowers the speed through the stat fold as before. A unit holding several is in every state any of them grants.

- **The cache and the gates.** `UnitAuras.Control` (World-side, internal) is the union of the held auras' `Control`
  flags, worked out again in `Record` whenever the changed aura or the current state has any (so a refresh onto a
  reloaded row that dropped its flags clears them) and in `Load`. `ControlGates` (`Avalon.World/Auras`) reads it with no
  scan, no clock and no allocation: `Of`, `CanMove` (not stunned or rooted), `CanTurn` (not stunned), `CanAct` (not
  stunned: items, doodads, gear, loot, NPCs, portals), `CanCast(unit, basic)` (not stunned, and not silenced unless the
  cast is the basic attack) and `IsBasic` (a row flagged `AbilityFlags.Basic`, or a creature's own basic). A unit that
  holds no auras is free. A state lasts until the aura pass removes its aura, every tick. The slow is never cached on
  the unit: only each copy's wire bit is.
- **Landing** (`AuraSystem.Apply`). On a **creature**, a crowd-control aura (one that stuns, roots or silences, or a
  slow) lands for `floor(duration x pct / 100)` (`CrowdControlRules.Scale`), the percentage being the smallest, among
  the types it grants, of the creature's `ControlResistance`. That is fixed at spawn (`CreatureSpawner`): the template's
  `StunDurationPct`, `RootDurationPct`, `SilenceDurationPct` or `SlowDurationPct` where set, else its rarity's
  `CreatureRarityModifiers` row, else 100; a `/reload creatures` reaches only creatures spawned after it. Slows now
  scale too: Crippled lasts 3 s on a Rare or a Boss. On a **character**, a stun, root or silence from another character
  takes its target's PvP diminishing step (`ControlDiminishing`, `CharacterEntity.Diminishing`, in memory only, never
  saved, so a new session starts clean): counted per type on the target, whoever cast it, the first lands at 100 %, the
  second at 50 %, the third at 25 % and the fourth is refused (`CrowdControlRules.DiminishPct`), and the count starts
  over once none of that type has landed for `Game:CrowdControlDiminishWindow` (default 6 s, above 0 and at most 7 s;
  [configuration](configuration-reference.md)). An aura granting several types takes the smallest step and records each.
  A creature's control on a character, an aura from nobody, and a slow alone never diminish. Every step that lands, a
  refresh included, is recorded and restarts the window; the refused step records nothing, so repeated tries cannot hold
  a target immune. **Nothing landing** (a resistance of 0, the refused step, or a creature walking home) is
  `AuraApplyResult.Resisted`: nothing is placed or recorded and the aura enters no combat (an ability's hit without
  damage has already entered combat when it landed, #1164); for an ability's aura its
  caster and whoever would have seen the hit get a hit of 0 marked `HitResult.Immune`
  (`ICombatOutcomes.ControlResisted`, sent as an evaded hit is), after the ability's own damage. No ability reaches a
  creature walking home, `Unavoidable` or not: its shape passes over it (#1181), so nothing is resisted and nothing is
  sent. Only crowd control applied to it directly, by a Resonance proc, is resisted and answered "Immune".
  Arrival
  protection, an invulnerable creature and a corpse still refuse it (`Refused`, no Immune). A doodad's or an item's
  application that is resisted counts as not applied. A refresh of a held crowd-control aura never shortens it: the copy
  keeps the later of its expiry and the new one, its `DurationMs` then being the time it had left.
- **Interrupts.** A stun or a silence that landed (and that a first hook did not end at once) ends the target's running
  cast bar through its instance (`AuraSystem`'s `controlled` callback, `MapInstance.Controlled`): a stun ends its
  ability cast (`IAbilityCastSystem.Interrupt`) and, for a character, its item and doodad cast bars; a silence ends its
  ability cast unless that is the basic attack or a cast an item started (`AbilityInstance.ForItem`), and no item or
  doodad bar. Only the wind-up ends, out loud, with the usual interrupt and its cast id: nothing fires, nothing is
  refunded and no cooldown starts, while a hit already fired and held for the dodge wait still lands and a projectile in
  flight flies on (unlike `CancelCasts`). It is safe from inside a cast firing on that tick (a stun dealt by a cast). A
  root interrupts nothing; for a character moving still does, and a rooted character does not move.
- **Characters.** Input while stunned or rooted (`PlayerInputHandler`) is taken but no step is: its sequence advances
  and the ack carries the unchanged position and zero velocity, so the client reconciles without drifting, and its
  velocity is set to zero; a rooted character turns to the input's yaw, a stunned one keeps its own.
  `CastAbilityHandler` refuses `CastRejectReason.Stunned` (15) right after `Dead`, and `Silenced` (16) once the ability
  is known, after `NotOwned`; the cast system refuses the same on both paths (`QueueAbility`, `RunInstant`), the one
  choke point for players, creatures and items, a cast an item started being spared by a silence. A stun refuses, each
  reply with an appended `Stunned` reason: an item use, gear included (`ItemUseResult.Stunned` 17, right after `Dead`),
  a drag or a destroy that touches Equipment or the Bank (`ItemRequestResult.Stunned` 13; Bag moves and destroying a Bag
  item still go), vendor buy, sell and buyback (`VendorResult.Stunned` 13, after `Dead`, before the leash), quest accept
  and turn-in (`QuestResult.Stunned` 11), a ground-loot pickup (`LootPickupResult.Stunned` 7) and a portal entry
  (`MapTransitionResult.Stunned` 14, asked at the request and again on arrival, after the instance is built). An open
  NPC conversation stays open. NPC and doodad interaction (`InteractHandler`; `DoodadUseService` as
  `DoodadUseRefusal.Stunned`) and dialogue choices are dropped silently, as their other refusals are. Cancelling one's
  own helpful aura (`CMSG_AURA_CANCEL`) is allowed. God mode does not lift crowd control.
- **Creatures.** `CreatureCombatScript` reads the cache once a tick as it engages: stunned, it stands as for a wind-up
  (a walk in progress stopped, no turn, no attack, no cast); rooted, it asks for no step and claims no new melee slot,
  but turns to and attacks a target in reach; silenced, it casts only its basic, ready and in reach (`ChooseAbility`,
  and so a forest rotation, is not asked: a rotation preferring a ready special would leave it attacking nothing).
  Neither stun nor root counts toward the 5 s unreachable give-up; the leash and the walk home are unchanged.
  `CreaturePatrolScript` takes no step while stunned or rooted: a leg in progress is stopped and asked for again once it
  is free, so its point is neither skipped nor counted reached. A slow needs nothing: the locomotion moves the creature
  at its slowed speed. The creature scheduler needs no hold: a fighting or walking creature is updated every tick, and a
  parked one reads its state when it wakes. Known limits: a rooted creature keeps the melee slot it already held for the
  root's few seconds; a crowd's separation can still push a controlled creature; a patrol's pause at a point runs on
  through crowd control.
- **Breaks** (`AuraRemoveReason.Broken` 6, World-side; a client hears an ordinary `Removed`). Two chances per aura row,
  in percent. `BreakOnDamagePct` is rolled once by each aura its holder holds whenever a hit or a periodic tick deals
  more than 0 to it and leaves it alive (`CombatService` reports `ICombatOutcomes.DamageTaken`, once per hit or tick, to
  `AuraSystem.DamageTaken`; the copies that break are ended after the walk). `BreakPerSecondPct` is rolled by the aura
  pass once for every whole second held (`ActiveAura.NextBreakRollAt`: a second after the copy was applied or renewed,
  then every second), never at or past its end, after its ticks and before its expiry. A late pass rolls every second it
  owes, as it pays ticks; the empty-instance skip, a hold (the loading screen, a move between instances) and a restore
  at select do not: the next roll keeps its place on the whole seconds held, so no burst of owed rolls follows. Every
  roll draws from the combat service's `ICombatRandom`, and a chance of 0 draws nothing. A hit on a unit holding no aura
  with a `BreakOnDamagePct` walks none of its auras: `UnitAuras.BreaksOnDamage` caches whether any held aura has one,
  worked out again by the same rule as `Control` (in `Record` when the changed aura or the current state has one, and in
  `Load`). Any aura row may carry the two chances; the seed gives them only to the crowd-control auras and the two slows.
- **Data.** World migration `SeedCrowdControl`: the aura columns above; on `CreatureRarityModifiers` four duration
  percentages, `StunDurationPct`, `RootDurationPct`, `SilenceDurationPct` and `SlowDurationPct` (0 immune to 100 the
  full duration, default 100; seeded Normal and Elite 100 each, Rare 50 each, Boss 0 stun, 50 root, 0 silence, 50 slow);
  the same four on `CreatureTemplates`, nullable (null takes the rarity's; none seeded). Check constraints
  `CK_CreatureRarityModifiers_<Type>DurationPct` and `CK_CreatureTemplates_<Type>DurationPct`, and
  `CreatureTemplateRules.Validate` and `ValidateRarities` (the Creatures reload area, the balance simulator, the API's
  edit), refuse anything outside 0 to 100. All are balance tables. The REST API edits the aura columns and the creature
  overrides ([live template editing](live-template-editing.md)); the rarity table has no REST surface. The client
  contract is [Aura protocol](aura-protocol.md), section 10.

## Resonance effects (#1180)

Worn gear places auras too: a character whose Resonance count for a theme reaches a threshold runs that theme's effect
rows ([item modifiers](item-modifiers.md#resonance-themes)), and each effect that fires applies its aura through the
instance's `AuraSystem.Apply`, as an ability's does. How the effects are activated and fired is in
[world simulation](world-simulation.md) (the Resonance effects); this section is what their auras are.

- **The effect auras** (World migration `AddResonanceEffects`, `ResonanceSeed`, balance data; `SeedIntegrityShould`
  pins them): 16-18 Burning I-III (harmful, 4 s / 1 s, damage 16, 22, 28), 19-21 Chilled I-III (harmful, 3 s,
  MovementSpeed Flat -15, -20, -25, and AttackSpeed Flat -8 on II, -12 on III), 22 Rimebound (harmful, 1 s, root),
  23-25 Jolt I-III (harmful, 200 ms / 200 ms, damage 14, 18, 22), 26-28 Regrowth I-III (helpful, 4 s / 1 s, heal 16,
  24, 32), 29 Rootsurge (helpful, 3 s / 1 s, heal 48), 30-32 Guard I-III (helpful, 4 s, Armor Flat 15, 25, 35,
  BlockPct Flat 2, 4, 5, and MaxWard Flat 25 on III), 33-35 Barbs I-III (harmful, 200 ms / 200 ms, damage 5, 8, 10),
  36 Bleeding (harmful, 4 s / 1 s, damage 20), 37-39 Tailwind I-III (helpful, 4, 4 and 5 s, MovementSpeed Flat 10, 12,
  15, and AttackSpeed and CastSpeed Flat 5 on II, 8 on III), 40 Slipstream (helpful, 2 s, MovementSpeed Flat 20),
  41-43 Veiled I-III (helpful, 3, 3 and 4 s, Evasion Flat 60, 100, 150, and MovementSpeed Flat 10 on II, 15 on III)
  and 44 Lucid (helpful, 4 s, CastSpeed Flat 10). Every damaging one is `Independent`, every other `Refresh`, all one
  stack; `PeriodicEffectiveness` is 0 on all, no row names a script, every one of their 26 modifiers is Flat, and the
  icon is the family's name in lower case, so the tiers of a family share one. Chilled breaks as the other slows do
  (0.5 % a second, never on damage) and Rimebound as Bound and Frozen do (25 % on damage, 0.5 % a second); it is not
  named Frozen because aura 14 is. Nothing but gear applies them: no seeded ability, doodad outcome or item script names one. They are
  ordinary auras in every other way: they tick, renew, break, save and restore, fold into the stats, replicate, and a
  helpful one can be cancelled, by the rules above.
- **One-tick bursts.** Jolt and Barbs last 200 ms with a 200 ms tick interval: the schedule anchored at expiry holds one
  tick, at the expiry, so each lands as one hit 0.2 s after its trigger and the aura ends with it. Being `Independent`,
  two wearers' bursts on one unit never renew each other.
- **The source** (`ResonanceEffect.Source`, built once per effect row when the items area loads, so a firing builds
  nothing): no ability id, a threat multiplier of 1 and no heal threat (a proc's damage counts for threat as a plain
  hit does, and a heal from gear adds none), no power gain, `Affects` `Ally` for an effect on the wearer and `Hostile`
  for one on another unit, and the row's damage type (Fire for Burning, Lightning for Jolt, Physical for Barbs and
  Bleeding; none on any other). For an aura that ticks it also carries a synthetic `AbilityMetadata` named for the
  aura, with tags `None` and no spell base, so the snapshot rolls nothing and its total is the aura's `PeriodicBase`
  scaled by the wearer's offence (`OffenceMath.PeriodicTotal`): a damaging aura by increased Damage, increased damage
  over time and its own type's increase (a Thorn barb or bleed takes increased physical damage, never Strength's
  increase to physical attacks, which only an Attack's physical part takes), a healing aura by increased healing.
  **Heal procs scale as heals do:** increased healing includes Intellect's share (`CombatFormula`'s
  `IncreasedHealingPerIntellect`, per point of Intellect), so Regrowth and Rootsurge grow with the wearer's Intellect
  as well as with its healing modifiers. A damage tick deals the row's type and meets that type's mitigation and the
  ward as any tick of it does (Burning and Jolt reach the ward first; a physical tick passes it).
- **`AuraSource.CannotCrit`** (#1180) is set on every effect's source: `AuraSystem.Apply` passes it to
  `AuraRules.Snapshot`, which keeps a crit chance of 0, so a proc's tick never crits whatever the wearer's crit chance.
  Each tick still draws its one crit roll, against that 0, so the order of draws is the same as for any other aura.
- **Both reloads hold the effects to their rules.** `ResonanceEffectCatalog` (`Avalon.World/Items`, built in the Items
  reload area's prepare against the aura rows read in that same prepare) leaves out, with an Error, a row whose aura
  is not loaded, a helpful aura on an enemy or a harmful one on the wearer, an aura that stuns, a damage type on an
  aura that deals none or none on one that does, and the row rules ([world simulation](world-simulation.md)). The
  catalog keeps its rows, so a `/reload auras` or a live edit (an aura edit reloads the Auras area alone) builds a new
  catalog of the same rows against the auras its prepare read (`ResonanceEffectCatalog.Against`, off the tick, logging
  at Error only a refusal the catalog it replaces did not already report for the same reason, so the startup load, a
  full reload, or `/reload items` then `/reload auras`, log each refused row once) and publishes it with them (`AurasPatch.Effects`, in place of the items generation's
  effects): an edit that gives Rimebound a stun leaves that effect out, and every kept effect's source is built again
  from its aura as it now is (an aura turned into a periodic heal scales by increased healing from then on). A
  character already online needs no stats refresh for it: its next firing finds its effects built from an older
  catalog and rebuilds them from the current one first ([world simulation](world-simulation.md)). The source still
  names `Affects`, so `AuraSystem.Apply`'s fit check, which refuses an aura turned helpful on an enemy or harmful on
  the wearer, logged at most once per aura per 10 s as `Aura {AuraId} '{AuraName}' is not applied: ...` (never as an
  ability, since the source has none), stays as a second guard.
- **On the wire** nothing is new: the effect auras reach clients through the aura list, update and tick packets, and a
  resisted Rimebound as the Immune hit of 0 with no ability id ([Aura protocol](aura-protocol.md), section 11).
